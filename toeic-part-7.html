READING TOEIC · PART 7

Reading Comprehension — Part 7

Read a variety of texts and answer questions about their meaning and details.

← All TOEIC parts

Part overview

What you'll practice

Locate information, understand purpose, make inferences and connect information across related texts.

Your task

How it works

Read the passage or group of passages, then answer questions using evidence from the text.

  • Skim first to understand the topic and organization.
  • Scan for names, dates, amounts and other details when needed.
  • For inference questions, choose what the text supports rather than what seems generally true.
