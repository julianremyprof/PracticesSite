LISTENING TOEIC · PART 1

Photographs — Part 1

Look at a photograph and choose the statement that best describes what is happening in the image.

← All TOEIC parts

Part overview

What you'll practice

Identify people, actions, locations, objects and relationships shown in a photograph.

Your task

How it works

Listen to four spoken descriptions and select the one that accurately matches the photograph.

  • Focus on the main action, not just the objects in the image.
  • Notice verb forms and prepositions that describe position or movement.
  • Be careful with statements that mention something visible but describe it inaccurately.
