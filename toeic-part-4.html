LISTENING TOEIC · PART 4

Talks — Part 4

Listen to short talks such as announcements, recorded messages and presentations.

← All TOEIC parts

Part overview

What you'll practice

Recognize the purpose of a talk, key details, speaker intent and information presented in order.

Your task

How it works

Listen to a short talk and answer questions about its main purpose and supporting details.

  • Identify the type of talk and its intended audience.
  • Listen for dates, times, locations and instructions.
  • Notice signposting expressions that introduce important information.
