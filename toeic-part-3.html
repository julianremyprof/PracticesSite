LISTENING TOEIC · PART 3

Conversations — Part 3

Listen to short conversations between two or more speakers and answer questions about what they say.

← All TOEIC parts

Part overview

What you'll practice

Identify the speakers' purpose, specific details, implied meaning and next actions.

Your task

How it works

Listen to a conversation and answer questions about its context, details and speakers' intentions.

  • Read the questions before listening when the activity allows it.
  • Listen for who is speaking and what each person needs.
  • Notice changes of plan, reasons and proposed next steps.
