SECTION 1 · BUILD YOUR APPROACH

Read for clues, not just words.

Every sentence gives you clues. Use them to predict what kind of answer belongs in the blank before you look at the options.

01

Read the whole sentence

Do not decide after seeing only the blank. The beginning and ending often reveal the meaning.

Clue: “By the time the meeting starts…” points to an action completed before a future moment.
02

Identify the missing role

Ask yourself: Do I need a verb, noun, adjective, adverb, preposition or connector?

Clue: An article + blank + “of the report” often signals a noun.
03

Check meaning and grammar

The answer must fit the sentence structure and make sense in the situation.

Clue: “Although” introduces contrast; “because” introduces a reason.

SECTION 2 · CHOOSE SMARTLY

Use the right strategy.

Look for the clue that separates the correct answer from the distractors.

GRAMMAR

Check the sentence structure

Look at the subject, verb form, tense markers and words before or after the blank.

The manager has _____ the proposal.

Correct: approved — “has” needs a past participle.

WORD FORMS

Find the part of speech

Use the words around the blank to decide whether you need a noun, verb, adjective or adverb.

The team worked _____ to meet the deadline.

Correct: efficiently — it describes how they worked.

VOCABULARY & CONNECTORS

Read for meaning and relationships

Think about the context, common word partnerships, contrast, cause and result.

The flight was delayed _____ heavy fog.

Correct: because of — it is followed by a noun phrase.

SECTION 3 · EXAM DAY

Train to succeed.

Good preparation combines accuracy, timing and a clear review routine.

1

Set a steady pace

Practise with the 20-second timer. Avoid spending too long on one difficult question.

2

Eliminate distractors

Remove options that break the grammar or do not fit the context. Compare the remaining choices.

3

Learn from every mistake

Read the explanation, name the clue you missed, and practise that skill again.

4

Repeat with a new set

Use all four drills to build consistency. Aim for accurate answers first, then improve your speed.

Remember: do not choose an answer because it “looks familiar.” Choose it because the sentence proves it is correct.
