SECTION 1 · BUILD YOUR APPROACH

Three decisions for every blank.

Do not choose an option just because it looks familiar. Find the evidence in the text.

01

Read the surrounding context

Read at least the sentence before and after the gap. Notice the document's purpose, audience and the relationship between ideas.

02

Identify what the blank requires

Decide whether you need a noun, verb, adjective, adverb, connector, phrase or complete sentence before comparing choices.

03

Eliminate incorrect options

Remove options that break the grammar, contradict the context or do not connect logically with the rest of the text.

Remember: Part 6 tests the whole text. A sentence may be grammatically correct on its own but still be the wrong answer because it does not fit the document's purpose or flow.

SECTION 2 · TRAIN FOR SUCCESS

Use the same routine every time.

Build a repeatable process that helps you balance speed and accuracy.

01 / SCANRecognize the text type, audience and purpose.
02 / READRead around each gap, not just the missing line.
03 / DECIDEPredict the missing function and eliminate distractors.
04 / CHECKRead the completed text to confirm grammar and flow.
