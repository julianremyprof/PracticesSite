Text Completion — Part 6

Read short written texts and choose the best words or sentences to complete them.

← All TOEIC parts

Part overview

What you'll practice

Use grammar, vocabulary, context and text organization to complete emails, notices and other short texts.

Your task

How it works

Read the surrounding text carefully and choose the option that best completes each blank.

  • Read the whole text to understand its purpose and audience.
  • Check how each option connects to the sentences before and after the blank.
  • Pay attention to pronouns, transitions and logical sequence.
