Question–Response — Part 2

Listen to a question or statement and choose the most appropriate response.

← All TOEIC parts

Part overview

What you'll practice

Understand the speaker's intention, identify question types and recognize natural replies.

Your task

How it works

Listen to one spoken prompt and choose the response that best fits its meaning and context.

  • Listen for question words such as who, where, when, why and how.
  • A natural response does not always repeat words from the question.
  • Watch for indirect answers, suggestions and polite requests.
