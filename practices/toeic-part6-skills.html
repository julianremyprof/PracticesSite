A THREE-STEP ROUTINE

Use this on every text.

Apply the same process while practising and when you sit the Reading test.

1

Read the surrounding context

Read the sentence before and after the blank. Identify the writer's purpose and how the ideas connect.

2

Identify what each blank requires

Check the sentence structure. Ask whether the gap needs a word, a phrase, a connector or an entire sentence.

3

Eliminate incorrect options

Cross out choices that break grammar or do not make sense. Confirm the remaining choice in the full text.
